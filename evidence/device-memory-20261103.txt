6. What the ADT can carry (host reading, not a hardware observation)
--------------------------------------------------------------------
The ADT generator of UniXNU takes one memory range (payload.dram_size, the DTS memory node) and DarwinBoot one window (darwinboot-memory-size in /chosen,
256 MiB to 8 GiB in 2 MiB steps). The kernel gets its memory from the window and the boot arguments, not from the DTS node; nothing that reads dram-base or
dram-size in /chosen was found in the kernel sources of this tree. So the 3.75 GiB bank below 0xf0000000 and the 4 GiB above 0x100000000 cannot both
be given to the kernel, and the 256 MiB at 0x2f0000000 that U-Boot reports are mapped by edk2 nowhere.

7. How the runs were made (no hands at the board)
-------------------------------------------------
Files on the ESP were replaced from the build host through the U-Boot prompt (uboot-put.py of the SoC repository sends the file with YMODEM over the console,
fatwrite writes it, fatload and crc32 verify it). Output for the committed unixnu.toml (all numbers hex, as U-Boot reads them):
uboot-put: put.bin, 1886 bytes sent; U-Boot after the transfer: Timed out | ## Binary (ymodem) download aborted
uboot-put: crc32 of the first 1886 bytes at 0x1000000: U-Boot 561e27eb, here 561e27eb
=> fatwrite nvme 0:1 0x1000000 EFI/UniXNU/unixnu.toml 0x75e
1886 bytes written in 2 ms (920.9 KiB/s)
=> fatload nvme 0:1 0x3000000 EFI/UniXNU/unixnu.toml
1886 bytes read in 1 ms (1.8 MiB/s)
=> crc32 0x3000000 0x75e
crc32 for 03000000 ... 0300075d ==> 561e27eb
The same for uefi-launcher.img (5251560 bytes, crc32 51ebc4c4) and kernel.macho.adt (14112 bytes, crc32 6f7e713f); the transfer of the launcher takes about 40 s at 1.5 Mbaud.
Then "run bootcmd" at the prompt; CAPTURE_ARGS="--send 'menu: shown' text:1 --send 'U-Boot v' ctrl-c,ctrl-c,... --stop '<INTERRUPT>' 1 'at the U-Boot prompt'"
typed the menu key, and stopped the capture with Ctrl-C at the next U-Boot banner (the watchdog or a panic resets the board).
