2. What the monitor showed
------------------------------
Reported by the person at the board while the xnu-hdmi run was up (it handed the kernel over at about 11:55): text scrolling on the monitor (the kernel log). Not
photographed; the picture quality (colours, position, readability) was not described beyond "scrolling".

3. The watchdog after the feeding stops (hdmi2 and the board host; area firmware)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------
[ 425.844] NativeNvme: wdt-kick: feed 360: count 0x78d8938b -> 0x7ffffffe (CR 0x9 STAT 0x0)
[ 430.846] NativeNvme: wdt-kick: 1800 s are over, not feeding the watchdog any more
# capture end 779.632 s, 876 raw bytes
hdmi2 started 11:40:54 (host clock), so "1800 s are over" was at about 11:48:04 and the DW watchdog (about 89 s) was due about 11:49:33.
Nothing more came through that capture: the FT232 adapter dropped off the bus and came back as /dev/ttyUSB1, and the capture kept reading the dead
device. Board host, 11:54:16:
  crw-rw---- 1 root dialout 188, 1 11月  3 11:49 /dev/ttyUSB1
  usb-FTDI_FT232R_USB_UART_<id>-if00-port0 -> ../../ttyUSB1        (link made 11月  3 11:49)
A later capture of the same line (hdmi3, started 11:54:42 on the by-id path) found the board in the boot loop: the first lines are a
probe-run kernel (the default xnu-probe entry) ending in the expected root-wait panic, and the restart:
[  14.388] NativeNvme: wdt-kick: feed 12: count 0x78d893da -> 0x7ffffffe (CR 0x9 STAT 0x0)
[  19.642] panic(cpu 4 caller 0xfffffff00798c150): Failed to mount root device @IOKitBSDInit.cpp:984
[  20.608] U-Boot v5-gc507c92ef837-261013-dirty #<builder> (Nov 03 2026 - 09:13:13 +0800)
So the board had been reset some time after the feeding stopped and then ran by itself. The reset was NOT seen on the serial line; that
it was the watchdog is an inference from the time (11:49, matching the 89 s) and from the adapter dropping at that moment.
