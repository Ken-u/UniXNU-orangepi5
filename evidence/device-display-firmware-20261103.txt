Host reading (not a hardware observation): the frame buffer is 1920 x 1080 x 4 bytes with a row of 7680 bytes = 8294400 bytes, inside the 0x7F0000-byte
"runtime data" run 0xE73D0000-0xE7BC0000 of the firmware memory map (section 6 of device-memory-20261103.txt lists that run), so it stays where it is after
ExitBootServices. Not recorded yet: the pixel order of the frame buffer, and the VOP2 registers (only the firmware's own log lines above).
